# Stakeholder Alignment

Maps the stakeholders around a decision or change, understands their interests and influence, and runs a deliberate alignment campaign. Alignment means honest persuasion and genuine incorporation of concerns, never deception or manipulation.

```
Skill("consulting-os-stakeholder-alignment", args="$ARGUMENTS")
```

## Examples

```
/consulting-os:stakeholder-alignment map who needs to agree before this goes to the board
/consulting-os:stakeholder-alignment plan how to pre-wire this decision
```
