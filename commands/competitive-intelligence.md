# Competitive Intelligence

Builds a structured picture of competitors: who they are, how they win, where they are vulnerable, and what they will likely do next. Uses only lawful, ethical sources — public information, published data, and transparently conducted interviews.

```
Skill("consulting-os-competitive-intelligence", args="$ARGUMENTS")
```

## Examples

```
/consulting-os:competitive-intelligence map the competitive landscape in payments
/consulting-os:competitive-intelligence build battle cards for our top three rivals
```
