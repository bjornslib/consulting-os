# Market Sizing

Sizes a market rigorously with top-down and bottom-up triangulation: TAM/SAM/SOM and growth outlook. Use this whenever a defensible market number is needed for a strategy, business case, pitch, or investor document.

```
Skill("consulting-os-market-sizing", args="$ARGUMENTS")
```

## Examples

```
/consulting-os:market-sizing size the collaboration software market for SMB in the US
/consulting-os:market-sizing what's the addressable market for our new product line
```
