# Risk Mitigation

Identifies, quantifies, and mitigates the risks to a strategy, transformation, or major decision, with owners, triggers, and pre-agreed responses. Use this when a plan or recommendation is heading to approval and needs its risk story.

```
Skill("consulting-os-risk-mitigation", args="$ARGUMENTS")
```

## Examples

```
/consulting-os:risk-mitigation build the risk register for this transformation
/consulting-os:risk-mitigation what could go wrong with this recommendation
```
