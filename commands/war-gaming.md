# War Gaming

Runs a competitive war game, simulating how competitors, regulators, and other players respond to a strategic move over multiple rounds, and hardens the strategy against those responses. Use this to stress-test a plan against reactions.

```
Skill("consulting-os-war-gaming", args="$ARGUMENTS")
```

## Examples

```
/consulting-os:war-gaming war-game how competitors respond to our price cut
/consulting-os:war-gaming stress-test this market entry against competitive reaction
```
