# Pricing Strategy

Designs or reviews pricing architecture, level, and metric, grounded in value, willingness to pay, and competitive position. Never facilitates price-fixing or collusion between competitors.

```
Skill("consulting-os-pricing-strategy", args="$ARGUMENTS")
```

## Examples

```
/consulting-os:pricing-strategy review our tiered pricing against willingness to pay
/consulting-os:pricing-strategy design a new pricing metric for this product
```
