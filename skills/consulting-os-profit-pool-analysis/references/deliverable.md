# Profit Pool Analysis — Output Template & Quality Bar

## Output template

```
## Pool boundary and cut
Definition; why this decomposition.

## Profit map
| Link | Revenue | Margin % | Profit $ | Share of pool profit |
Plus the revenue-vs-margin chart description.

## Why the shape exists
Barriers protecting fat pools; forces that thinned the thin ones.

## Migration
Where profit is shifting, drivers, 3–5 year view.

## Implications for the client
Which pools they sit in, which they could reach, what it would take.
```

## Quality bar

- Margins are triangulated, not asserted; allocation logic for multi-link players is stated.
- Absolute profit is shown, not just margin percentages.
- Every fat pool has a named barrier; "they're just good" is not an explanation.
- The migration section makes a falsifiable directional claim.
