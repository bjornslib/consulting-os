# War Gaming — Output Template & Quality Bar

## Output template

```
## Game definition
Move under test, horizon, battleground, win conditions per player.

## Player briefs
Objectives / economics and constraints / decision style / move set.

## Round log
Per round: moves, responses (with in-character rationale), adjudicated outcomes and the adjudication logic.

## Findings
Moves that survived / dangerous responses and their early signals / third-player effects.

## Hardened strategy
Modifications adopted; trigger → pre-agreed response table.
```

## Quality bar

- Competitors were played from their economics and incentives, not as straw men.
- Adjudication logic is written down and consistent across rounds.
- At least one uncomfortable finding survived into the output — a war game where the client wins every round was mis-played.
- Findings became concrete triggers and prepared responses, not colour commentary.
