# The Reusable Operating System

The 21 skills are not a syllabus to be run in order; they are a kit. This meta-skill selects the right subset for the engagement at hand, sequences them, keeps the shared artefacts consistent as work proceeds, and — the part most consultancies skip — harvests every engagement into IP that makes the next one cheaper and better.

## The suite at a glance

**Frame** 01 problem-framing → 02 fact-base → 03 assumption-audits *(03 also re-runs before any major recommendation)*
**Analyse** 04 market-sizing · 05 competitive-intelligence · 06 customer-segmentation · 07 profit-pool-analysis
**Decide** 08 strategic-options · 09 pricing-strategy · 10 business-cases · 11 portfolio-review
**Design** 12 operating-model-design · 13 initiative-prioritisation · 14 transformation-roadmaps
**Protect** 15 risk-mitigation · 16 war-gaming · 17 kpi-architecture
**Carry** 18 stakeholder-alignment *(runs continuously from Decide onwards)* · 19 executive-narrative · 20 board-memos

## Engagement patterns

Select by engagement type; adapt rather than obey:
- **Growth strategy**: 01 → 02 → 04 → 06 → 07 → 05 → 08 → 10 → (16) → 19/20, with 03 and 18 woven through.
- **Pricing engagement**: 01 → 02 → 06 → 05 → 09 → 16 → 10 → 19.
- **Transformation / AI adoption**: 01 → 02 → 12 → 13 → 10 → 14 → 15 → 17 → 18 → 19, 03 before approval.
- **Portfolio / investment review**: 01 → 02 → 07 → 11 → 08/10 → 20.
- **Rapid diagnostic (1–2 weeks)**: 01 → 02 (thin) → the single most relevant analytical skill → 19. Say what was *not* done.

For any engagement: confirm the pattern with the user before starting, then track which skills have run and which artefacts exist.

## Engagement hygiene (the connective tissue)

- **Shared artefacts stay consistent**: one problem statement, one fact base, one assumption register, one risk register. When a downstream skill changes a number, the change propagates back — a business case using different market numbers to the sizing work is an integrity failure.
- **The 03 gate**: no recommendation goes to a decision-maker without an assumption audit on record.
- **The 18 thread**: from the moment options exist, stakeholder alignment runs in parallel, not as an afterthought before the final meeting.
- **Provenance**: every deliverable can trace each claim to the fact base or the assumption register.

## Harvest: turning engagements into reusable IP

At engagement close (or milestone), extract four asset classes:
1. **Sanitised frameworks** — issue trees, option structures, KPI trees, and models with client data removed, filed by industry and problem type.
2. **Benchmarks and reference data** — non-confidential market sizes, margin references, ratios, with dates and shelf-life noted. Respect confidentiality obligations absolutely: client-confidential data is never harvested, only the reusable *structure* of the work.
3. **Prompt and method library** — the prompts, analyses, and sequences that worked, written so the next engagement starts from them (this is the appendix the suite accumulates over time).
4. **Lessons register** — what the pre-mortem missed, which assumptions failed, which estimates were off and by how much. Feed these back as calibration into skills 03, 04, and 10.

Store harvested assets where the user directs; propose updates to the individual skills themselves when a lesson generalises — the suite is designed to be edited.

## Quality bar

- The engagement plan names skills *not* being used and why — scope discipline is part of the plan.
- Shared artefacts are single-sourced; no forked numbers.
- Harvest happened before the team scattered; confidentiality was respected in what was kept.
- At least one skill in the suite got improved per engagement.
