# Risk Mitigation — Output Template & Quality Bar

## Output template

```
## Register (ranked, top 8–12 only)
| Risk | Source | Impact ($/time/share) | Likelihood | Exposure | Treatment | Cost of treatment | Owner |

## Ruinous-tail risks
Low-probability/kill-shot items handled outside expected-value ranking.

## Trigger table
| Risk | Early signal | Watcher | Pre-agreed response |

## Review rhythm
Cadence, reporting format, escalation route.
```

## Quality bar

- Impact is in client units; no all-medium heat maps.
- Every risk has a real treatment; "monitor closely" appears nowhere.
- Mitigation costs are stated and compared to exposure.
- Owners are individuals; triggers are observable.
