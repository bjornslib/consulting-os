# Business Cases — Output Template & Quality Bar

## Output template

```
## The ask and the answer
Investment requested; NPV / payback / peak funding per scenario; recommendation.

## Driver model summary
Driver tree, key inputs with source and grade.

## Scenarios
Base / downside / upside as coherent stories; break-even point.

## Sensitivity
Tornado; the 2–3 swing inputs; de-risking actions for each.

## The case against
Strongest objections, steel-manned; evidence that would confirm them.

## Decision requested
Exactly what approval, funding, and conditions are sought.
```

## Quality bar

- No hard-coded growth rates masquerading as drivers.
- The downside scenario is believable, not decorative.
- Swing inputs have de-risking actions, not just acknowledgement.
- The case against would satisfy the smartest sceptic in the room.
