# Stakeholder Alignment — Output Template & Quality Bar

## Output template

```
## Decision and network map
The decision; stakeholders with role in this decision.

## Stakeholder register
| Person | Position (evidence) | Interests (org + personal) | Influence | What movement requires |

## Campaign plan
Sequenced interactions: who, when, by whom, purpose, material needed.

## Objection log
Objections surfaced → adopted / addressed / respectfully declined (with reason communicated).

## Status
Current alignment picture; remaining blockers; next moves.
```

## Quality bar

- The map reflects the real network, not the org chart.
- Positions are evidence-based; inference is labelled.
- Opposed stakeholders were engaged early, not managed around.
- At least one substantive change to the recommendation came from the process — pre-wiring that changes nothing was ceremony.
