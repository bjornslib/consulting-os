# Kpi Architecture — Output Template & Quality Bar

## Output template

```
## KPI tree
Outcome → drivers → initiative-level metrics (diagram or nested list), mapped against the strategy.

## KPI specifications
| KPI | Definition/formula | Lead/lag | Source | Owner | Target (basis) | Thresholds | Counter-metric |

## Gaming analysis
Per vulnerable KPI: the exploit, the paired counter-metric.

## Tiered views
Board / executive / functional slices.

## Operating rhythm
Review cadence, forum, escalation rules.
```

## Quality bar

- The tree traces to the strategy's driver logic — not a list of what happens to be measurable.
- Leading indicators exist for every critical lagging outcome, with stated causal hypotheses.
- Targets have a stated basis; owners can actually influence their metric.
- Gaming analysis was done; naked vulnerable metrics were paired or cut.
