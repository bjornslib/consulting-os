# Competitive Intelligence — Output Template & Quality Bar

## Output template

```
## Market map
Clusters, players per cluster, one-line "how they win" each.

## Competitor profiles (top 3–6)
Facts / Economics / Revealed strategy / Vulnerabilities / Likely moves.

## Benchmark table
Dimensions customers weight × players, with our client's position.

## Implications
3–5 "so what" statements for the client's strategy.
```

## Quality bar

- Every profile ends in a prediction, not just a description.
- Vulnerabilities are structural (hard for them to fix), not cosmetic.
- Sources are dated and cited; rumour is labelled rumour.
- The "so what" section could brief a partner in five minutes.
