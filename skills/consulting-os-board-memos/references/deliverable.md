# Board Memos — Output Template & Quality Bar

## Output template

```
## Board paper
1. The ask and why now (exact decision text)
2. Context (≤1 page)
3. The case (logic, evidence, economics)
4. The case against (steel-manned)
5. Risks and mitigations (top 5, quantified, owned)
6. Alternatives considered
7. Governance: approvals, conditions, conflicts, report-back date

## Draft resolution
Minute-ready wording.

## Appendices list
Model, full risk register, supporting analysis.
```

## Quality bar

- The ask is on page one and the resolution is minute-ready.
- The case against would satisfy the board's toughest member.
- Numbers reconcile exactly with the business case; risks match the register.
- A report-back commitment is included.
